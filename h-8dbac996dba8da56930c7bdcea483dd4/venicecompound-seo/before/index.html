The Venice Compound

Just Sold  ·  $14,500,000

The highest Venice residential sale since 2017

  • 8 Beds
  • 5.5 Baths
  • 6,719 SF

The Venice Compound, 637 and 649 Palms Boulevard, is a restored Craftsman property reimagined by Electric Bowery. It sits on a rare double lot on one of the most desirable streets in Venice, moments from the shops and restaurants of Abbot Kinney.

Four detached structures are set across landscaped grounds. Each can function on its own while remaining connected to the rest, suited to multigenerational living, guest accommodations, creative studios or wellness spaces.

Layered courtyards run through the property, framed by drought-tolerant landscaping and a series of gathering spaces, for relaxed daily living and larger entertaining.

Cedar shake siding, custom stained glass and bespoke millwork pay homage to the original Craftsman design, refined for a more modern way of living.
