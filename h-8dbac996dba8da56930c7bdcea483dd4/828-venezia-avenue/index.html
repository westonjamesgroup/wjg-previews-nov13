Venice · Modern Residence

828 Venezia Avenue

For sale$3,995,000

Behind a gate on Venezia Avenue, a few blocks from Abbot Kinney Boulevard, the late Patrick Killen, AIA, left part of the upper floor open, so that light falls through to the living spaces below and the two storeys read as a single volume. Outdoor rooms follow at every height, from grade to the roof.

From the entry, the ground floor runs unbroken to dining and living, where a fireplace is set into polished concrete and warm wood. Walls of glass give onto patios at the front and side. The kitchen gathers around a central island with a wine refrigerator, and a separate office sits apart from the main room.

Upstairs, each of the three bedrooms has its own bath. The primary suite adds a walk-in closet and a bathroom with a steam shower and freestanding tub. Laundry, linen storage and two balconies share the level. A last flight of stairs leads up to the deck, among the palms.

Past the yard, a detached two-car garage with a glazed sliding door doubles as a workspace or gym.

Patrick Killen, AIA

The Architect

Killen founded Studio 9one2 in Manhattan Beach and, from the 1980s, was one of a handful of architects bringing a serious Modernism to the South Bay's narrow coastal lots. His Sherin House, on the Strand, took its cues from the lifeguard towers and has been called bold and unprecedented. The work was collected in books such as The New 100 Houses and The World’s Best Beach Houses.
