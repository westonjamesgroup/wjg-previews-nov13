MEET THE FOUNDER

Weston Littlefield

With more than $550 million in career sales, Weston Littlefield has established himself as a leading figure in Los Angeles luxury real estate, specializing in architecturally significant residences, exceptional estates, and exclusive off-market opportunities. His portfolio includes some of Southern California’s most celebrated architectural works, including John Lautner’s Garcia House, Richard Neutra’s Tremaine House and William H. Levit House, Craig Ellwood’s Case Study House #16, Wallace Neff’s Straus House, and Cliff May’s Dr. Richard Henry House. Beyond architectural estates, his track record includes 20 beach-lot sales along Malibu’s La Costa Beach and Carbon Beach, alongside a $39 million residential sale that began with a direct message on social media.

Recognized among The Hollywood Reporter’s 37 Top L.A. Real Estate Brokers in 2026 and named to the Los Angeles Business Journal Leaders of Influence, Weston has earned a reputation for exceptional representation and an unwavering commitment to his clients. Known for his personable, intuitive approach, he combines market expertise, discretion, and a profound appreciation for architecture to deliver a highly personalized real estate experience built on trust, integrity, and lasting relationships.
