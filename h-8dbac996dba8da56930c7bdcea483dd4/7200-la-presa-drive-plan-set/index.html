7200 La Presa Drive is a 0.97-acre site of 42,253 square feet in Outpost Estates, in the Hollywood Hills, with views of the city and the ocean.

The site is offered with approved plans and permits issued by LADBS in July 2026, RTI prior to issuance, for a gated contemporary residence: an 8,807 SF residence, including basement, with a 713 SF attached three-car garage and a 526 SF detached ADU. The residence rises two stories over the basement, with a pool, spa and pool cabana.

The plans show five bedrooms in the main house, a library and a study on the ground floor, and a roof deck on the upper level. Interior and pool images are concept renderings.
