With more than $550 million in career sales across Southern California, Weston has established himself as a leading force in luxury real estate, with a distinct specialization in architecturally significant residences and exceptional properties.

His portfolio includes some of the region’s most celebrated architectural landmarks, including Richard Neutra’s Tremaine House, John Lautner’s Garcia House, and Craig Ellwood’s Case Study House No. 16, reflecting a deep appreciation for architectural heritage and a proven ability to represent properties of extraordinary significance.

His track record includes 20 transactions exceeding $10 million, along with 20 beachfront lot sales along Malibu’s coveted Carbon Beach and La Costa Beach, underscoring his expertise in Southern California’s most exclusive coastal markets.

As founder of Weston James Group at AKG | Christie’s International Real Estate, Weston combines an intimate understanding of architecture, strategic market insight, and a highly personalized approach to representing distinguished properties and discerning clientele.
